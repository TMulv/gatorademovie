The Story

BORN IN THE HEAT

Gainesville, Florida. 1965. Dr. Robert Cade watches his players collapse in the August heat and asks a question no one else thought to ask: why don't they come back from the bathroom?

What starts as curiosity becomes obsession. Against skeptical administrators, dismissive coaches, and a sports establishment that thinks water is enough, Cade races to build something that has never existed. A drink engineered for the human body under maximum stress.

But science doesn't make history. Someone has to believe in it first.
